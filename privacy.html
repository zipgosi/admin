We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers we need to do the work (for example, our email, accounting and equipment suppliers), and when the law requires it.

We keep business records as long as needed for the work and for tax and legal reasons. You can ask us to correct or delete your information by email, and we will do so unless we must keep it by law.
